A specialist creative agent

How can an AI act inside visual software without being given an unbounded computer?

I led the system design and weekly productization loop; we built it as a small cross-functional team. User intent becomes a plan, curated actions modify the product, and visual evidence informs what happens next.

Our main discovery was that intelligence is distributed. The model matters, but so do the harness, tool contracts, deterministic software, local perception, image engine, native interface, and evaluation loop. No single layer is sufficient by itself.

My role
Team lead, architecture, productization, evaluation, and release coordination
Team shape

What I understood

  1. 01

    Typed tools and native interactions are a product architecture, not merely an integration detail.

  2. 02

    Visual evidence closes the loop between an agent’s decision and the real state of the product.

  3. 03

    Evaluation becomes useful when it informs the next product decision, not when it only produces a score.

DailySurf

How can a session log become a useful memory of progression without becoming administrative work?

DailySurf is a mobile companion for surfers: log a session while it is happening, remember the conditions, and make progress visible over time.

As founding CTO and primary product engineer, I shaped the product and built its mobile architecture around fast capture, offline-aware writes, privacy-conscious location, and a calm path from session to reflection.

An internal support copilot

What does useful assistance look like when context, adoption, and accountability matter as much as the answer?

I led a support copilot grounded in ticket context and company knowledge, then designed the observation and handover loops needed for it to become part of daily work.

We treated impact as an observational question, not an easy causal claim. The framework connects assistant use to support outcomes while accounting for cohort and case complexity, and keeps final responsibility with the support professional.

My role
System design, impact framework, adoption, and operational handover
AI role
Retrieve context, draft advice, and support the operator
Human role
Interpret the case, judge the response, and own the customer interaction
