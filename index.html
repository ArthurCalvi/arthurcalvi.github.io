About

I’m Arthur. I lead a small team of AI engineers at DxO Labs.

Our work spans several areas, but I would describe it simply: we rethink products, processes, and parts of the organisation so that people and AI can work through the same systems. We build internal tools, evaluation loops, engineering processes, and new features for photographers.

One strand of that work is a visual agent for creative software. I use vibe retouching as shorthand for expressing a visual intent in ordinary language while an agent translates it into a bounded, inspectable sequence of actions. The aim is to shorten the path to a first editable result without replacing craft or direct control.

Outside DxO, I co-founded DailySurf, where I work as CTO and lead product engineering. DailySurf is a mobile app for surfers to record their sessions and follow their progress over time.

Previously: mathematics and machine learning at ENS Paris-Saclay, followed by remote-sensing work at Kayrros, CNRS, and Inria.
