Hello and welcome

I’m Arthur, born and raised in Marseille (Allez l’OM!). After preparatory classes I moved to Paris to study at École Normale Supérieure. I began in civil engineering, then switched to mathematics and completed the MVA master (Mathématiques, Vision, Apprentissage).

I started my career in deep learning applied to remote sensing — first at Kayrros as an intern, then a PhD at INRIA/LSCE. I didn’t enjoy the day‑to‑day of research (I did write two papers), and around that time I tried ChatGPT for the first time. It felt like a revolution: a compressed interface to the world that you can query in natural language.

I began building tools for my research and realized I wanted to build full‑time. I met Jérôme Ménière, CEO of DxO Labs, and joined as a GenAI consultant, prototyping tools to streamline internal processes. Today I lead the GenAI Transformation group — a small team deploying AI across the company and into DxO’s products.

Outside work, I split time between the Alps and Corsica and love the outdoors — surf, skiing, and mountain biking. I also used to be a Halo geek. This site is a simple place to share some of my work and essays.
