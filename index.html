Arthur Calvi

I lead the AI Acceleration Team at DxO Labs. We are a small team working across products, engineering, and internal tools.

We build AI features, help other teams use AI in their work, and sometimes change a process so that both people and AI can use it. I tend to think of AI as either an executor or an adviser. People still define the problem, design the system, and decide whether the result is acceptable.

We are still learning where this division works and where it does not. This website is where I write about some of that work, along with earlier research in machine learning and remote sensing.

Previously: mathematics and machine learning at ENS Paris-Saclay, followed by remote-sensing work at Kayrros, CNRS, and Inria.

Projects

Some DxO projects are described without their names because they are not public.

A visual agent for creative software

At DxO, we are building an agent that can work inside creative software. It turns a request into a plan, uses a limited set of actions in the product, and looks at the result before deciding what to do next.

An internal support assistant

We built an assistant that uses ticket context and company knowledge to help support staff investigate and draft answers. The support person remains responsible for the reply.

pr-preflight

A repository tool that runs deterministic checks and focused AI reviews on a pull request. It catches routine problems before human review, where attention can stay on architecture, risk, and exceptions.

DailySurf

Outside DxO, I build DailySurf, a mobile app for surfers to record sessions, remember the conditions, and follow their progress over time.
