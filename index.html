01 · Working thesis

What we are discovering

Useful AI is rarely one model or one prompt. It emerges from the way context, tools, interfaces, evaluation, and human judgment fit together.

  1. 01

    Start with the whole system

    Rethink the process, the information it carries, and the decisions it produces—not only the task given to a model.

  2. 02

    Give AI bounded ways to act

    Clear tools, typed interactions, and visible evidence make an agent more useful than an open-ended interface.

  3. 03

    Keep judgment visible

    AI can execute and advise. People still set intent, design constraints, read risk, and decide what is ready.
