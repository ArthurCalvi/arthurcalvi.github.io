My PhD focused on detecting, segmenting, and classifying forest disturbances from satellite imagery. I worked on combining heterogeneous disturbance datasets, localising events in space and time, and using forest phenology to distinguish seasonal variation from genuine forest change.

The broader disturbance work remained unfinished and unpublished. I have documented the graph-based dataset framework, the tree-phenology research, and why I decided to leave the PhD.
